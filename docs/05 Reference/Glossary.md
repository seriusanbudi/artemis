# Glossary

## Agent glasses

A general-purpose observation tool that shows a business how an AI agent
discovers, identifies, understands, and describes it using publicly available
information.

## Agent visibility

The observable ability of an AI agent to find, identify, understand, and
recommend a business for a relevant task.

## Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Practices intended to improve how content or entities appear in systems that
directly answer questions rather than returning only a list of links.

## Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

Practices intended to improve visibility or representation in AI-generated
answers supported by retrieval and synthesis.

## Branded discovery

A test where the business name, domain, social handle, or another direct
identity clue is included in the prompt.

## Unbranded discovery

A test where the prompt describes a category, problem, or customer need without
naming the target business.

## Entity resolution

The process of determining which names, websites, profiles, locations, and
mentions refer to the same real-world business.

## Reference profile

The business profile automatically extracted from the official website and used
to evaluate whether an agent's claims align with the business's published
information. In V1 this is not independently verified real-world ground truth.

## Source attribution

The process of identifying and classifying the sources associated with an
agent's answer or a business fact.

## Recommendation visibility

The frequency and prominence with which an agent recommends a business for a
relevant customer intent.

## Agent share of voice

The target business's share of mentions or recommendations relative to selected
competitors across a defined test set.
