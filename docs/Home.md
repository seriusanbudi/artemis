# Artemis

> [!abstract] Product thesis
> AI agents are becoming gatekeepers between customer intent and businesses.
> Artemis measures whether agents can find, understand, trust, and recommend a
> business—and explains the evidence behind their behavior. It acts as
> **agent glasses** for any type of business.
